Topic 1: My Identity

Goal: introduce yourself naturally, describe your background and personality, and talk about your interests and goals.

2. Essential Verbs

  • be — I am a teacher.
  • live — I live in Ireland.
  • come from — I come from China.
  • work — I work in a hospital.
  • study — I study English every day.
  • learn — I'm learning English.
  • enjoy — I enjoy reading books.
  • prefer — I prefer tea to coffee.
  • speak — I speak English and Chinese.
  • introduce — Let me introduce myself.
  • meet — Nice to meet you.
  • become — I want to become a better speaker.
  • improve — I'm improving my English.
  • choose — I chose this career.
  • decide — I decided to study abroad.
  • hope — I hope to travel more.

6. Mini Dialogue

A: Hi! Nice to meet you.

B: Nice to meet you too.

A: Could you tell me a little about yourself?

B: Sure. My name is David. I come from Ireland, and I live in Galway. I work as a software engineer. In my free time, I enjoy reading books and learning English. One of my goals is to become a more confident English speaker.

7. Speaking Practice

  1. What's your name?
  2. Where are you from?
  3. Where do you live?
  4. What do you do?
  5. What are your hobbies?
  6. What languages do you speak?
  7. What are you learning now?
  8. Why are you learning English?
  9. What are your goals?
  10. What kind of person are you?

8. Writing Practice

Write a 100–150 word self-introduction. Try to include at least 10 new words, 5 collocations, and 5 sentence patterns from this topic.
